About CHAMBERS OF ABHINAV SINGH

Legal Practice in Delhi

Abhinav Singh appears in civil appeals and special leave petitions before the Supreme Court of India. His High Court work includes writs, arbitration petitions and bail applications.

His district court practice extends across Saket, Rohini, Karkardooma, Tis Hazari, Dwarka and Patiala House. He also appears before the National Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission.

The practice also covers cheque dishonour, family court proceedings and consumer disputes.

Services & Practice Areas

Three integrated practice pillars, structured to address the full spectrum of legal and financial challenges.

01Commercial and civil recovery
Commercial Recovery: Abhinav Singh represents clients in commercial recovery matters involving civil suits and execution applications before the High Court of Delhi and various district courts. These matters arise under the Indian Contract Act, Specific Relief Act, and Companies Act, focusing on enforcing contractual and corporate obligations.
Civil & Miscellaneous Litigation: He appears in contempt petitions and appeals in the High Court of Delhi and other forums. His work involves matters governed by the Code of Civil Procedure, Limitation Act, and Indian Contract Act, addressing disputes requiring judicial intervention in civil proceedings.
Criminal Litigation: Abhinav Singh handles bail applications and criminal complaints in the High Court of Delhi and Karkardooma Court. His practice includes matters under the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, and Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, involving criminal procedural and substantive law.
Writ & High Court Matters: He appears in civil and criminal writ petitions primarily before the High Court of Delhi. These matters engage constitutional provisions under the Constitution of India, focusing on fundamental rights and legal remedies through writ jurisdiction.
Arbitration: Abhinav Singh represents parties in arbitration-related petitions before the High Court of Delhi. His work involves matters under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act and the Code of Civil Procedure, addressing enforcement and challenge of arbitral awards.

Motor Accident Claims: He appears in appeals related to motor accident claims before district courts. These matters involve claims for compensation arising from road accidents and related legal proceedings.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where does Abhinav Singh appear?

He appears before the Supreme Court of India, the High Court of Delhi, Delhi district and family courts, and the National Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission.

Can you help register my startup?

Absolutely. We handle the full incorporation process for Pvt Ltd, LLP, OPC, and Section 8 entities, including PAN, TAN, GST, and sectoral registrations.

Do you handle UPRERA registrations?

Yes. We assist real estate agents and developers with UPRERA agent registration, project registration, and ongoing quarterly compliance filings.

Can you file my GST returns?

Our in-house CA team manages GST registration, monthly and quarterly filings, and handles GST notices and assessments on your behalf.

Does the Chambers handle cheque dishonour matters?

Yes. Abhinav Singh appears in cheque dishonour matters under the Negotiable Instruments Act.

Do you handle matrimonial and divorce cases?

Our family law team handles divorce, legal separation, child custody, maintenance, and domestic violence matters with sensitivity and strategic precision.

What is the Virtual CFO service?

Virtual CFO provides growing businesses with strategic financial leadership on a retainer basis—covering budgeting, audit oversight, investor relations, and financial reporting.
