Services & Practice Areas

A multidisciplinary litigation practice encompassing commercial disputes, civil and criminal proceedings, constitutional remedies, arbitration and appellate advocacy. The Chambers’ professional portfolio extends across the Supreme Court of India, the High Court of Delhi, district courts and specialised adjudicatory forums.

01Commercial Litigation & Recovery

The Chambers undertakes commercial litigation arising from contractual obligations, financial liabilities, payment defaults and business transactions. Its practice encompasses commercial recovery suits, execution proceedings and matters involving dishonoured negotiable instruments before the High Court of Delhi and various district courts. The scope of representation extends from the adjudication of financial disputes to the enforcement of legally recognised obligations and judicial decrees.

  • Commercial and contractual disputes
  • Recovery of outstanding payments and financial liabilities
  • Commercial and civil recovery suits
  • Execution and enforcement of civil decrees
  • Cheque dishonour proceedings under the Negotiable Instruments Act, 1881
  • Disputes arising from legally enforceable debts
02Civil & Miscellaneous Litigation

The Chambers maintains a diverse civil litigation practice encompassing disputes concerning individual rights, civil obligations and enforceable legal interests. Its professional portfolio includes civil suits, appeals, contempt petitions and miscellaneous judicial proceedings. The practice also extends to consumer dispute proceedings before the National Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission and matters relating to motor accident compensation claims.

  • Civil suits and appellate proceedings
  • Contempt petitions and miscellaneous civil applications
  • Disputes concerning civil rights and obligations
  • Consumer protection and redressal proceedings
  • Representation before the NCDRC
  • Motor accident compensation-related proceedings
  • Enforcement of judicial orders and decrees
03Criminal Litigation

The Chambers represents parties in criminal proceedings before the High Court of Delhi and various district courts. Its practice encompasses bail applications, criminal complaints and miscellaneous criminal petitions, addressing matters involving criminal liability, procedural safeguards and statutory remedies. The practice also includes criminal complaint proceedings arising from cheque dishonour under the Negotiable Instruments Act, with due consideration of the applicable substantive, procedural and evidentiary framework.

  • Bail applications
  • Criminal complaints and related proceedings
  • Criminal miscellaneous petitions
  • Matters involving criminal liability and procedural safeguards
  • Criminal proceedings under the Negotiable Instruments Act
  • Statutory and procedural remedies under applicable criminal laws
04Constitutional Law & Writ Petitions

The Chambers undertakes civil and criminal writ proceedings, primarily before the High Court of Delhi, addressing matters involving constitutional protections, statutory entitlements and the exercise of public authority. Its practice encompasses proceedings concerning judicial review, administrative decisions and the enforcement of legal and constitutional rights. Abhinav Singh’s professional background in government representation further constitutes a relevant dimension of his engagement with public law.

  • Civil and criminal writ petitions
  • Constitutional and statutory remedies
  • Judicial review of administrative action
  • Matters involving government and public authorities
  • Proceedings concerning fundamental and legal rights
  • Challenges to administrative and governmental decisions
05Arbitration & Dispute Resolution

The Chambers undertakes representation in arbitration-related judicial proceedings arising from commercial arrangements and contractual relationships. Its practice encompasses petitions before the High Court of Delhi concerning the statutory framework governing arbitration, judicial intervention and proceedings relating to arbitral awards. This area of practice brings together commercial litigation and the legal mechanisms governing arbitration under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996.

  • Arbitration-related petitions
  • Commercial and contractual disputes involving arbitration
  • Judicial intervention in arbitral proceedings
  • Proceedings concerning challenges to arbitral awards
  • Enforcement-related proceedings concerning arbitral awards
  • Matters under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996
06Supreme Court & Appellate Practice

The Chambers undertakes appellate representation before the Supreme Court of India and the High Court of Delhi, encompassing civil appeals, Special Leave Petitions and challenges to judicial decisions. Its professional portfolio is distinguished by Abhinav Singh’s appointments as a Panel Counsel for the Government of India, Senior Counsel for the Government of Uttar Pradesh and Counsel for the Government of Madhya Pradesh before the Supreme Court of India. These appointments constitute a significant aspect of his professional experience in appellate advocacy and government representation.

  • Special Leave Petitions (SLPs)
  • Civil appeals and appellate proceedings
  • Representation before the Supreme Court of India
  • Challenges to judicial decisions
  • Proceedings involving questions of law and statutory interpretation
  • Central and State Government representation
