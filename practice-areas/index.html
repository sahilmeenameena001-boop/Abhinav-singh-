Services & Practice Areas

Three integrated practice pillars, structured to address the full spectrum of legal and financial challenges.

01Commercial and civil recovery
Commercial Recovery: Abhinav Singh represents clients in commercial recovery matters involving civil suits and execution applications before the High Court of Delhi and various district courts. These matters arise under the Indian Contract Act, Specific Relief Act, and Companies Act, focusing on enforcing contractual and corporate obligations.
Civil & Miscellaneous Litigation: He appears in contempt petitions and appeals in the High Court of Delhi and other forums. His work involves matters governed by the Code of Civil Procedure, Limitation Act, and Indian Contract Act, addressing disputes requiring judicial intervention in civil proceedings.
Criminal Litigation: Abhinav Singh handles bail applications and criminal complaints in the High Court of Delhi and Karkardooma Court. His practice includes matters under the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, and Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, involving criminal procedural and substantive law.
Writ & High Court Matters: He appears in civil and criminal writ petitions primarily before the High Court of Delhi. These matters engage constitutional provisions under the Constitution of India, focusing on fundamental rights and legal remedies through writ jurisdiction.
Arbitration: Abhinav Singh represents parties in arbitration-related petitions before the High Court of Delhi. His work involves matters under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act and the Code of Civil Procedure, addressing enforcement and challenge of arbitral awards.
